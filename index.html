Momo Baby Tracker - Account Deletion

This page explains how users can permanently delete their Momo Baby Tracker account and associated cloud data.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open Momo Baby Tracker.
  2. Sign in using your Google account.
  3. Go to: Settings → Account Center → Delete Account
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.
  5. Press Delete Account.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • User Profile
  • Baby Profiles
  • Milk Records
  • Breastfeeding Records
  • Sleep Records
  • Diaper Records
  • Temperature Records
  • Growth Records
  • Food Records
  • Medicine Records
  • Calendar Records
  • Custom Records
  • Uploaded Photos

Retention

All cloud data is permanently deleted immediately after account deletion. No personal cloud data is retained after deletion.

Need Help?

If you experience any issue deleting your account, please contact the developer through the email address listed on Google Play.
