Momo Baby Tracker - Account Deletion

This page explains how to delete your Momo Baby Tracker app sign-in and the cloud parenting data that the App supports deleting, and what is not automatically removed.

We do not claim that every copy of every data type is erased everywhere. Some records are retained for security, and some data stays on other devices or outside the App.

How to Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open Momo Baby Tracker and sign in with Google (if you are not already signed in).
  2. Open the Member Center (for example: tap the membership chip such as Free member / 免費會員 on the home or Settings header).
  3. Tap Permanently delete account / 永久刪除帳號.
  4. Read the warnings, then tap Next.
  5. Type DELETE to enable confirmation, then tap Delete account only if you intend to proceed.

Path summary (current App UI, version 1.0.155+320):
Member Center → Permanently delete account → type DELETE → confirm

What the App Deletes (Supported Scope)

When deletion completes successfully, the App removes:

  • Your Momo Baby Tracker app sign-in (Firebase Authentication user for this App)
  • Cloud parenting data under your App user that the App supports deleting (for example: baby profiles and care logs stored in the App’s Firebase Cloud Firestore user space, such as feeding, sleep, diaper, temperature, growth, food, medicine, calendar/custom events, notifications, and related profile / membership status documents the App is designed to wipe)
  • Local App data on this device (cleared as part of the in-App deletion flow)

The exact cloud collections wiped by the current App runtime are the user subcollections the deletion service is built to clear (including legacy billing-related cleanup documents under the same user). This page describes that supported wipe scope; it does not promise deletion of data the App cannot reach.

What Is Retained or Not Automatically Removed

  • Minimal security lock record: after deletion starts, the App keeps a minimal deletion-lock record (account identifier / UID and timestamp). It does not contain parenting care logs. Purpose: reduce the risk that an old device writes cloud data again for a deleted account.
  • Other devices: local data on other phones/tablets is not cleared automatically. Please sign out or uninstall the App on those devices.
  • Exports and backups: files you previously exported or saved outside the App (PDF/CSV/backup archives, copies in Downloads/cloud drives, etc.) are not deleted automatically.
  • Your Google Account: deleting the App sign-in does not delete your Google Account itself.
  • Google Play subscriptions: account deletion does not cancel a Google Play subscription. If you subscribed, cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) to avoid further charges.
  • Diagnostics / crash reports: diagnostic data already sent to service providers (for example Firebase Crashlytics) may remain according to that provider’s retention policy. The App does not promise immediate erasure of all historical diagnostic copies.
