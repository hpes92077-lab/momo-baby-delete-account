Momo Baby Tracker — Account & Data Deletion

本頁說明如何在 萌萌育兒記／Momo Baby Tracker 中： (1) 保留登入帳號並刪除個別育兒紀錄；以及 (2) 永久刪除 App 登入帳號與 App 支援刪除的雲端育兒資料，以及哪些資料不會自動清除。

This page explains how to (1) keep your sign-in and delete individual care records in Momo Baby Tracker, and (2) permanently delete your App sign-in and the cloud parenting data that the App supports deleting, and what is not automatically removed.

我們不主張每一種資料的每一份副本都會在所有地方立即消失。部分紀錄會為安全目的保留，部分資料可能留在其他裝置或 App 以外的位置。
We do not claim that every copy of every data type is erased everywhere. Some records are retained for security, and some data stays on other devices or outside the App.

保留帳號刪除育兒紀錄
Delete care records without deleting your account

中文

  1. 您可以保留 Google／Firebase 登入帳號，不必刪除帳戶。
  2. 在 萌萌育兒記 App 中開啟您要刪除的育兒紀錄，選擇刪除並確認。
  3. 可逐筆刪除 App 支援刪除的紀錄，例如：餵奶、尿布、睡眠、親餵、體溫、散步、副食品、行程／提醒等。
  4. 若該筆紀錄已同步至 Firebase Cloud Firestore，App 會在同步時刪除對應的雲端文件。若裝置離線或同步失敗，雲端刪除可能延後，待之後成功同步再完成。
  5. 本 App 不提供「保留帳號、一鍵清空所有雲端育兒資料」的功能。
  6. 設定中的「清除所有本機資料」只清除本機，不等於刪除雲端資料；若之後仍以同一帳號同步，雲端紀錄可能再次下載到裝置。
  7. 若寶寶已有歷史紀錄，「刪除寶寶」可能改為封存並保留相關紀錄，而非永久清除該寶寶的全部資料。
  8. 若需要刪除整個 App 登入帳號及其關聯的雲端／本機資料，請依下方「永久刪除帳號」流程操作。
  9. 如需協助，請聯絡：contact@mujunda.com

English

  1. You may keep your Google／Firebase sign-in. You do not need to delete your account.
  2. In the Momo Baby Tracker App, open the care record you want to remove, choose delete, and confirm.
  3. You can delete individual records the App supports deleting, such as feeding, diaper, sleep, breastfeeding, temperature, stroll／walk, solid food, and schedule／reminder entries.
  4. For records already synced to Firebase Cloud Firestore, the App deletes the matching cloud documents during sync. If the device is offline or sync fails, cloud deletion may be delayed until a later successful sync.
  5. The App does not provide a “keep account, one-tap wipe all cloud parenting data” feature.
  6. “Clear all local data” in Settings clears local data only. It is not cloud deletion. If you sync again with the same account afterward, cloud records may download back to the device.
  7. If a baby profile already has history, “delete baby” may archive the baby and keep related records instead of permanently clearing all of that baby’s data.
  8. To delete the entire App sign-in and associated cloud／local data the App supports wiping, follow the permanent account-deletion steps below.
  9. For help, contact: contact@mujunda.com

如何在 App 內永久刪除帳號
How to Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open Momo Baby Tracker（萌萌育兒記）and sign in with Google (if you are not already signed in).
    開啟 App，並以 Google 登入（若尚未登入）。
  2. Open the Member Center (for example: tap the membership chip such as Free member / 免費會員 on the home or Settings header).
    開啟會員中心（例如點首頁或設定上方的會員狀態標籤）。
  3. Tap Permanently delete account / 永久刪除帳號.
  4. Read the warnings, then tap Next.
    閱讀警告後點選「下一步」。
  5. Type DELETE to enable confirmation, then tap Delete account only if you intend to proceed.
    輸入 DELETE 以啟用確認，確認無誤後再點選刪除帳號。

Path summary (current App UI; wording may vary slightly by language／theme):
路徑摘要（目前 App 介面；文案可能因語系／主題略有差異）：
Member Center → Permanently delete account → type DELETE → confirm
會員中心 → 永久刪除帳號 → 輸入 DELETE → 確認

永久刪除帳號時，App 會刪除的範圍
What the App Deletes (Supported Scope)

When account deletion completes successfully, the App removes:
當帳號刪除成功完成時，App 會移除：

  • Your Momo Baby Tracker app sign-in (Firebase Authentication user for this App)
    您的 萌萌育兒記 App 登入（此 App 的 Firebase Authentication 使用者）
  • Cloud parenting data under your App user that the App supports deleting (for example: baby profiles and care logs stored in the App’s Firebase Cloud Firestore user space, such as feeding, sleep, diaper, temperature, growth, food, medicine, calendar/custom events, notifications, and related profile / membership status documents the App is designed to wipe)
    App 支援刪除的、屬於您帳號底下的雲端育兒資料（例如存放於 Firebase Cloud Firestore 的寶寶資料與照護紀錄等）
  • Local App data on this device (cleared as part of the in-App deletion flow)
    此裝置上的本機 App 資料（作為 App 內刪除流程的一部分清除）

The exact cloud collections wiped by the current App runtime are the user subcollections the deletion service is built to clear (including legacy billing-related cleanup documents under the same user). This page describes that supported wipe scope; it does not promise deletion of data the App cannot reach.
實際清除的雲端子集合以現行 App 刪除服務設計為準（含同使用者底下的舊版帳單相關清理文件）。本頁僅說明支援清除的範圍，不承諾刪除 App 無法存取的資料。

刪除帳號後仍會保留、或不自動清除的項目
What Is Retained or Not Automatically Removed

  • Minimal security lock record／最小安全鎖定紀錄: after deletion starts, the App keeps a minimal deletion-lock record (account identifier / UID and timestamp). It does not contain parenting care logs. Purpose: reduce the risk that an old device writes cloud data again for a deleted account.
    刪除開始後，App 會保留最小鎖定紀錄（帳號識別碼／UID 與時間），不含育兒照護紀錄內容，用以降低舊裝置再次寫回雲端的風險。
  • Other devices／其他裝置: local data on other phones/tablets is not cleared automatically. Please sign out or uninstall the App on those devices.
    其他手機／平板上的本機資料不會自動清除；請在該裝置登出或移除 App。
  • Exports and backups／匯出與備份: files you previously exported or saved outside the App (PDF/CSV/backup archives, copies in Downloads/cloud drives, etc.) are not deleted automatically.
    您先前匯出或另存於 App 外的檔案不會自動刪除。
  • Your Google Account／您的 Google 帳號: deleting the App sign-in does not delete your Google Account itself.
    刪除本 App 登入不會刪除您的 Google 帳號本身。
  • Google Play subscriptions／Google Play 訂閱: account deletion does not cancel a Google Play subscription. If you subscribed, cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) to avoid further charges.
    刪除帳號不會自動取消 Google Play 訂閱；如有訂閱請至 Google Play「付款和訂閱」取消，以免繼續扣款。
  • Diagnostics / crash reports／診斷／當機報告: diagnostic data already sent to service providers (for example Firebase Crashlytics) may remain according to that provider’s retention policy. The App does not promise immediate erasure of all historical diagnostic copies.
    已送出之診斷資料可能依服務提供者保留政策繼續保存；App 不保證立即清除所有歷史診斷副本。
